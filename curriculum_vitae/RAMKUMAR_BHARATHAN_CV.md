## RESEARCH & EVALUATION (OPEN-SOURCE AI BENCHMARKS & ASSURANCE)

- **ControlTrace (Pilot — [github.com/ramtoo-cell/controltrace](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/controltrace)):** Six audit and compliance investigation cases with PCAOB AS 1105 / ISA 500 ground truth and named traps (*salience*, *accusation-is-not-evidence*, *calibrated uncertainty*), plus minimal pairs that flip between resolve and escalate on a single piece of corroborating evidence. Built through an agentic Claude workflow where I define the construct and trap taxonomy, review and correct each case and its ground truth, and verify the scorer. A blind Claude baseline reached the right outcome in **5 of 6** cases, yet cited evidence it should have excluded in **5 of 6** cases, and in **1 of 6** (`CT-001`) treated an unsubstantiated competitor complaint as proof of a violation after computing that a vendor's disclosure had lapsed >3 years earlier. *Current limits: six cases, one model, one sample per case, ground truth not yet independently validated.*
- **Conceptual Reasoning & Calibration Evals ([frontier-reasoning-bench](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/frontier-reasoning-bench) · [policy-reasoning-bench](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/policy-reasoning-bench) · [reasoning-eval-harness](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/reasoning-eval-harness)):** Built deterministic invariant verifiers across economics, law, policy, philosophy, and cognitive science, alongside 72 structured reasoning cases and calibration/abstention harnesses (`reasoning-paired-evals`, `reasoning-calibration-audit`, `reasoning-rubric-reliability`).
- **Agentic Governance & Runtime Assurance ([agentgate](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/agentgate) · [agent-guardian](https://github.com/ramtoo-cell/agent-guardian)):** Authored a ~90-page control playbook for an AI-first company (including agentic-identity and CI/CD controls), open-source MCP tool-call policy proxies with SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledgers, and technical defensive publications on **TD Commons** covering AI and controls.
